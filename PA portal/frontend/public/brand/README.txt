Drop your own brand assets here. Anything present overrides the built-in
default automatically — no code change, just reload the page.

  logo          the Nam Kural wordmark, shown in the nav
  school        plate for the School Education card
  tamil         plate for the Tamil Language, Culture and Archaeology card
  information   plate for the Information and Publicity card
  film          plate for the Film Technology and Cinematograph Act card
  valluvar      Thiruvalluvar figure in the questionnaire's couplet bar (transparent PNG best)

Any image extension works: .png .jpg .jpeg .webp .svg .avif
So "film.png" and "film.jpg" are both fine — the basename is what matters.

If a file is absent the app falls back to the Wikimedia Commons image it
ships with. Cards using your own image drop the Wikimedia photo credit.

Tip: a logo with a transparent background sits best in the nav. If yours has
a solid backdrop, it can be keyed out — ask and it will be done for you.
